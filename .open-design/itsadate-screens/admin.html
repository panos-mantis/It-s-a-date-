2 · Reject, asked first

Reject "Sunday pastry walk"?

The author will see it as not approved. It stays off Home, and you can still change it from All ideas.

A real role="alertdialog" with aria-modal, labelled by its title and described by its body. Focus starts on Cancel (the safe choice), Tab stays inside, Escape or Cancel closes it and puts focus back on the row's Reject button, and the page behind is inert. "Reject idea" sends PATCH /admin/ideas/:id {"status":"rejected"}; the row leaves the queue and a toast says "Idea marked as not approved." with Undo. The danger fill is used for this confirm button only. Approve doesn't ask: it can be undone from its toast.

3 · Delete a tag, asked first

Delete "Board games"?

It comes off every idea that has it, and the Board games button leaves Home. An idea tagged only Board games is left with no tag until someone edits it. This can't be undone.

DELETE /admin/tags/:id pulls the tag off every idea before it deletes it (tags.repository.ts), so the copy says so. The API can't count the ideas that have a tag, so the dialog doesn't give a number. No Undo: a deleted tag can't come back with its ideas. Afterwards a toast says "Tag deleted" and focus goes to the next row's Rename button.

Maya tapped the heart on "Golden-hour viewpoint drive": the card leaves at once (DELETE /ideas/:id/save), the Saved count drops to 2, and focus moves to the heart of the card that took its place (the one before it if it was last, the Saved panel if none are left). The toast sits at the bottom centre in the page's one live region, which comes straight after the tab panels, so Undo is the next Tab stop after the cards. It stays 8 seconds, longer while hovered or focused, and Escape closes it. Undo (PUT /ideas/:id/save) puts the card back in the same place, focuses its heart and says "Back in your saved ideas." If the request fails, the card comes back and the toast says "We couldn't remove that. Try again." The app's Toast has no action yet: Phase 5 adds one.
